## The challenge

Online food ordering needs to feel as immediate and inviting as the food itself. Losos needed a website that could present a broad sushi menu clearly while keeping the path to checkout short and easy to understand.

## The solution

The Losos website combines rich product presentation with a focused ordering flow. Customers can browse the menu, open a dish for more details, add items to their order, and move through checkout without losing context.

The layouts use clear hierarchy, generous imagery, and responsive components so the experience stays fast and easy to use whether someone is choosing dinner on a laptop or ordering from their phone.

## Result

The project delivered a polished restaurant storefront that gives Losos a strong digital presence and a practical foundation for menu updates, online orders, and future customer experiences.
