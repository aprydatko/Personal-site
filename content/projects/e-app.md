## The challenge

Teams working across websites and mobile apps need a reliable way to keep customer information, leads, and ongoing work connected. Without a shared CRM, important context becomes fragmented between channels and teams.

## The solution

E-app brings customer management and everyday CRM operations into one focused platform for web and mobile. Teams can review customer details, move leads through their workflows, and manage the settings that shape their day-to-day process.

The interface uses clear hierarchy and compact, task-oriented layouts so teams can understand the current state at a glance and take the next action without unnecessary steps.

## Result

The project established a scalable CRM foundation for website and mobile-app businesses: organized customer data, visible lead workflows, and a consistent product structure that can adapt as the team grows.
