## The challenge

Wow Sushi needed a digital storefront that could match the energy of its brand while making local delivery feel quick and uncomplicated. Customers should be able to move from a visual menu to a complete order with minimal friction.

## The solution

The website puts the sushi menu at the centre of the experience. Customers can explore categories, open product details, build a cart, and find the delivery information they need before placing an order.

The interface combines bold food imagery with clear navigation and responsive layouts, keeping the same straightforward ordering experience available on desktop and mobile screens.

## Result

The project delivered a lively online ordering experience for Wow Sushi, giving the restaurant a stronger digital storefront and a practical foundation for menu updates, local delivery, and future customer features.
